Account

Delete your account

Zyron POS accounts belong to a pharmacy and its staff. You can remove one staff login yourself, or ask us to erase the whole pharmacy account.

Last updated 12 November 2026 · Android app com.wormhole.zyron

In the app · immediate

Remove a staff login

The shop owner can do this at any time.

  1. Open Zyron POS and go to Settings → Staff & roles.
  2. Find the staff member and tap Delete.
  3. Confirm. That login stops working straight away.

The app keeps a disabled entry so the same login cannot sign in again. To have that entry erased too, email us as described below.

By email · within 30 days

Delete the whole pharmacy account

  1. Email support@wormhole.co.in from the shop. Include the shop name and the registered phone number.
  2. We confirm the request with the shop owner.
  3. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and email you when it's done.
Email a deletion request

Delete some data, keep the account

The shop can delete customers, medicines and other records itself inside the app. To have any other data removed, for example one customer's history or all lab records, without closing the account, email support@wormhole.co.in with the shop name and what to remove. We do it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • All staff sign-in accounts for the pharmacy
  • The shop's cloud data: medicines, stock, suppliers, customers, loyalty points, lab records and logs
  • Bill links the shop shared on WhatsApp

What is kept, and for how long

Copies of sale and purchase invoices may have to be kept for the period Indian GST and drug-sale rules require. During that time they are stored securely, used for nothing else, and then deleted.

Data stored on the shop's own phones and computers is under the shop's control. Uninstalling the app removes it from that device.

Questions

Write to support@wormhole.co.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
